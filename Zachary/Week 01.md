# PROG Week 01 — What programming is

Teaching week 21–25 Sep 2026. Mon 21 Sep 16:00–18:00 and Thu 24 Sep 09:00–11:00, rooms 4.32 and 4.33. Syed Murad, group 2.

The only week 1 file is a workbook of questions. It does not contain the answers. In class you answer them alone, then in a group of 3 to 5 you present "Object-oriented programming, four OOP principles, and the history of Python". The four principles are not defined in this file, or in the week 2 and week 3 slides that were exported.

## Questions the workbook asks

1. What is computer programming?
2. What are the fundamental concepts of computer programming?
3. What are the different types of programming languages?
4. What are the characteristics of high-level and low-level programming languages?
5. What is machine language?
6. What is assembly language?
7. What is object-oriented programming?
8. What are the concepts in object-oriented programming?
9. What is the difference between procedural and object-oriented programming?
10. Why do syntax and semantics matter?
11. What is the difference between a compiled language and an interpreted language?

Learning objectives printed on the workbook: programming and its basic ideas, high-level against low-level languages, syntax and semantics, object-oriented programming, and procedural against object-oriented.
